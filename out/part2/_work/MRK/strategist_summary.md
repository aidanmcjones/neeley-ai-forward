# MRK strategist summary (Part 2, Decide)

## Position (from out/part1/MRK/part1.json)
1. Keytruda is 49% of 2025 sales [MRK:10k_FY2025.md p.31]; U.S. Keytruda ($18,829M) is 29.0% of $65,011M, and it faces biosimilars from December 2028 plus IRA price setting from January 1, 2029 (finding 5).
2. Keytruda Qlex (U.S. patent to 2043) was 7.9% of U.S. Keytruda-family sales in Q2 2026 against a 30% to 40% adoption target; Medicare pays a 3.6% per-dose premium for Qlex over IV (product table, growth limiter).
3. In Europe a Halozyme injunction has blocked SC Keytruda in Germany since December 2025, with a Dutch counterclaim covering seven more markets pending (finding 3).
4. Balance sheet: an undisclosed-on-calls IRS transition-tax NOPA, about $1.3B tax plus $260M penalties before interest (finding 1, sharpest needle), and a long-dated inventory pool up from $3.8B to $5.9B (finding 2).
5. U.S. chargeback and rebate provision fell $13.3B to $10.0B on the Januvia list-price reset (finding 4); Januvia is now generic and IRA-priced.

## My ranking
1. MRK-m01: the only move that touches the 2028 to 2029 cliff at scale with a cited erosion comp; but the direction is already announced (30% to 40% target, "priced to drive adoption"), so its novelty is only the funded increment and the 40% hard goal. Skeptics should judge that increment.
2. MRK-m04: every operand cited, tiny cost, not announced anywhere; modest return and the pool's contents are unknown.
3. MRK-m02: not announced and tied to near-dated catalysts (Nov 19, 2026 hearing, Dutch ruling), but thin return because SC and IV share the 2031 EU expiry and Merck is winning on validity.
4. MRK-m03: large certain cost against assumed elasticity; useful mainly as the rejected alternative to m01.
5. MRK-m05: built on the sharpest needle but it is risk housekeeping, not a strategic move, and the return rests on an uncited probability.

Suggested rejected alternatives for the deck if m01 is picked: m03 (price cut costs about 5x the campaign for the same assumed lift) and m02 (overpays for a dispute Merck is winning).
