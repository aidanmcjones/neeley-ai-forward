# AMGN strategist summary (Part 2)

## Position in five lines (from out/part1/AMGN/part1.json)
1. Finding 1 (Sharpest Needle): a $2.4B TAVNEOS intangible ($2.3B U.S.) sits under an FDA withdrawal proposal; the Q2 2026 10-Q recoverability wording changed and no call states the carrying value.
2. Finding 2: sales deductions rose to 54% of gross product sales in 2025 from 52%, and the reserve build (partly "timing of payments") flattered 2025 operating cash flow by about $1.07B.
3. Finding 3: Q1 2026 TEPEZZA growth included a 22% inventory impact (about $84M), mostly a one-quarter growth-quality issue.
4. Finding 4: the IRS Puerto Rico case is at least $7.6B before interest net of repatriation credits; Tax Court decision expected no earlier than late 2026.
5. Finding 5: profit-share payments to UCB (EVENITY) and AstraZeneca (TEZSPIRE) rose $349M to $1,308M in 2025; product table: Repatha has the broadest label in its class but guides net price down mid-single digits as oral Lipfendra arrives.

## Candidates (cost, return; all math in candidates.jsonl)
- AMGN-m01 (other:regulatory_evidence, finding 1): offer the FDA a new randomized, independently adjudicated TAVNEOS confirmatory trial plus liver registry as PMRs. Cost about $74.6M and 40 FTE over 24 months. Return: about $112.9M expected avoided operating-income loss over 24 months (15-point retention uplift [assumption]; break-even 9.9 points), plus about $345M expected non-cash impairment avoided. Already announced: partly (hearing, RWE and re-adjudication are announced; a new trial is not).
- AMGN-m02 (campaign, finding 2 plus product table): $120M label-only HCP campaign on Repatha's outcomes indication before Lipfendra scales. Return: about $175.5M U.S. revenue over 24 months (about $115.5M contribution, near break-even). Already announced: partly (management says education is needed). Promo piece and claims map drafted; label section 6 not indexed.
- AMGN-m03 (license_or_acquire, finding 5): buy out UCB's EVENITY profit share and EU rights for about $3.6B (5x 2025 payments [assumption]). Return: about $1,703.4M pre-tax operating income over 24 months net of forgone interest. Not announced. No buyout comp is indexed.
- AMGN-m04 (pricing_or_coverage, finding 2): up to 5 extra rebate points on U.S. Repatha (about $195M over 24 months) only for removal of prior authorization (about 80% of 318 covering Part D formularies require PA). Return: about $175.5M net revenue over 24 months at a 10% lift [assumption]; break-even lift 5.26%. Already announced: partly (past net-price cuts for access).

## My ranking
1. AMGN-m01: the only move aimed at the Sharpest Needle, it is cheap relative to a $2.3B asset, and it is not announced. The skeptic's best attack is that the FDA may not accept a PMR offer.
2. AMGN-m03: the largest return, and it is driven by an audited, fast-growing payment stream. It ranks second because the price has no comp and UCB's view is unindexed.
3. AMGN-m04: it targets a measured barrier (PA share from CMS) at modest cost and has a clean kill signal in the next SPUF release. The volume response is assumed.
4. AMGN-m02: it is partly obvious (management already frames the label gap) and only breaks even inside 24 months.
