## Sharpest Needle

Row 1 (PFE-c01). No PFE ruling is both highly material and non-obvious, so this is the least-bad choice: c01 is the only high-materiality survivor and every operand is printed in three filers' 10-Qs, but it quantifies a share loss management and rivals have already described, so it is credited as a quantification, not a discovery. The less discussed c04 (legal charge and Depo-Provera wording) ranks below it because it is GAAP-only, medium materiality and its link to Depo-Provera is an inference.
