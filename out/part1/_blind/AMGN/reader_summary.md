# AMGN blind read: reader summary

## The company in five lines
1. 2025 revenue was $36.75B (+10%) and product sales $35.15B. Growth came from volume (+13%) while net price fell 3% (10-K FY2025 p.63).
2. Six growth brands (Repatha, EVENITY, TEZSPIRE, rare disease, innovative oncology, biosimilars) are offsetting Prolia/XGEVA biosimilar erosion (-32% to -34% in Q2 2026) and IRA price setting on ENBREL (2026) and Otezla (2027).
3. Gross-to-net is heavy and rising. Sales deductions took 54% of gross sales in 2025, up from 52%, driven by 340B mix, Part D redesign and IRA.
4. Footnote risks are concentrated. The IRS Puerto Rico case ($7.6B net before interest for 2010-2015) is still open, and a 2016-2018 NOPA and a 2019-2022 audit now extend it. TAVNEOS ($2.4B intangible) is under an FDA withdrawal proposal.
5. On policy, Amgen is among the least exposed to IRA, has tariff relief for about three years without a forward investment pledge, and does a substantial majority of its commercial manufacturing in Puerto Rico.

## Three strongest candidates
1. **AMGN-c05 (Footnotes): the IRS dispute escalated in 2026.** The Tax Court decision slipped from "second half of 2026" to "late 2026 or early 2027", and the court ordered supplemental briefs. The 2016-2018 NOPA was reissued twice (May and July 2026), and the audit team's method is harsher than the IRS's own trial position. Exposure for 2010-2015 is $10.7B assessed ($7.6B net of credits) plus interest, against about $6.3B of UTB-related accruals, or about $14/share. Only the draft NOPA was said on a call.
2. **AMGN-c06 (Footnotes): TAVNEOS intangible at risk.** Amgen carries $2.3B of U.S. carrying value, 5.6 times the product's roughly $0.43B annual sales. The FDA proposal alleges untrue statements in the original application, and the Q2 2026 note adds "observations with respect to new U.S. patient interest". A full write-off would cost up to about $4.2 GAAP EPS pre-tax. Calls do not give the carrying value.
3. **AMGN-c08 (Earnings-call Q&A) together with AMGN-c02 (Gross-to-net): disclosure got thinner as reserves started driving growth.** After the SEC's April 2024 letter, Amgen told the SEC it would "refine the level of detail" on calls. Since then, net-price mentions on calls fell from 5-6 per call to 0-2. Meanwhile 10-Q true-ups of estimated sales deductions now move brand growth by up to 20 points (ENBREL +16% in Q2 2026), and about 1.5 points of Q2 2026 product growth came from these true-ups.

Also worth carrying forward:
- **AMGN-c03:** TEPEZZA U.S. sales fell 4% in 2025, and 22 points of its 29% Q1 2026 growth was channel inventory.
- **AMGN-c07:** EVENITY and TEZSPIRE profit share rose 36% to $1.31B, so a third of their incremental sales went to partners.

## Product chosen
Repatha vs Praluent, Leqvio and Lipfendra. Repatha has the broadest label: CV-event reduction for any adult at increased risk, HoFH from age 10, and no food rules. Leqvio and Lipfendra are LDL-only. Even so, Repatha's net price fell 7% in Q1 2026 and is guided down by mid-single digits.
