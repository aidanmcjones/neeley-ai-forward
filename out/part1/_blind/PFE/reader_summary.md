# PFE blind reader summary

## The company in five lines

1. Revenue was $62.6B in 2025 (down 1.6%) and $29.5B in H1 2026 (up 4%). COVID products are fading out (Paxlovid Q2 2026: $21M). (10-K FY2025 file p.58; 10-Q 2026Q2 p.5, p.39)
2. Growth comes from Eliquis, Padcev, Lorbrena, Nurtec and the Vyndaqel family. Ibrance ($4.1B) is flat to down and takes a Medicare negotiated price 50% below list on 1 Jan 2027.
3. GAAP Q2 2026 was a loss of $0.04 per share. It carried $4.3B of impairments, $842M of legal charges and a $550M H1 step-up in Metsera contingent consideration. Adjusted EPS was $0.77.
4. The MFN/tariff agreement is final, and Pfizer's tariff rate is zero until 20 Jan 2029. The FY2025 pricing risk factor now names TrumpRx, CMMI pilots and cuts to ACA subsidies and Medicaid.
5. Product revenue deductions are 42% of gross sales and widening. Chargebacks are the fastest-growing line.

## Three strongest candidates

- **PFE-c01 (Peer contrast, high confidence).** Vyndaqel's U.S. share of the ATTR-CM class fell from about 70% to about 51% in one year, using combined U.S. class revenue (Q2 2025 to Q2 2026).
  - Vyndaqel U.S.: +$74M.
  - Amvuttra U.S.: +$448M.
  - Attruby: +$151M.
  - Vyndaqel U.S. was flat in H1 2026 ($1,975M vs $1,976M).
  - Sources: PFE, ALNY and BBIO 10-Qs for 2026Q2.
- **PFE-c03 (Earnings-call Q&A).** U.S. Vyndaqel fell in Q3'25, Q4'25 and Q1'26.
  - Over the same period, the 10-Q driver language moved from "IRA Part D Redesign" to "new payer contracts".
  - The CFO still called Vyndaqel a "solid contribution" in the Q1 2026 call (p.5).
  - No executive has said how large the U.S. price concession is.
  - c10 (label) explains the structural part: Amvuttra is given by a healthcare professional, so Medicare Part B pays for it and it avoids the Part D redesign.
- **PFE-c02 (Gross-to-net).** Deductions went from 38.6% of gross sales (2024) to 41.3% (2025) to 42.3% (H1 2026).
  - Q2 2026 chargebacks: +11.8%.
  - Q2 2026 returns: +95%.
  - Annual cost: about $0.5-0.6B of revenue (about $0.10 pre-tax EPS).
  - Gross-to-net and chargebacks are almost never mentioned on the calls. When the 340B question was asked on the Q2 2026 call, it was deflected (c11).

Also worth weighing:
- **c09:** Ibrance gets its 50% Medicare cut one year before Kisqali and Verzenio, and Ibrance had zero mentions in the last four calls.
- **c05:** Metsera contingent consideration rose from $1.79B to $2.26B.
