## Sharpest Needle candidate

**NVO-nugget-001, the rebate float behind H1 2026 cash.** Novo told the market H1 free cash flow rose 44% to DKK 55.3bn and raised the full-year guide. Its balance sheet shows where the cash came from. Remove the non-cash USD 4.2bn 340B reversal from the opening balance, and the US sales-deduction liability grew DKK 33.6bn in six months, about 61% of H1 FCF, while adjusted sales fell 2% in DKK. The company's only explanation is a clause: "timing of US rebate payments". Its own full-year guide (DKK 45-55bn) already implies H2 FCF of minus 0.3 to minus 10.3bn. The next leg is dated too: on 1 Jan 2027 Wegovy and Ozempic list prices fall 50% and 35% to USD 675, and the 6-K says this "is expected to impact Novo Nordisk's cash flow in 2027". Rebates are paid 100 to 150 days after the sale, so a lower list price shrinks the accrual and the float is repaid in cash. It beats the runner-up, the DKK 130.6bn depreciation wall (003), because 001 needs no assumption: it is arithmetic on three balance sheets and the company's own guide. 003 rests on an assumed useful life and on how much of Kalundborg is already running. The weakness of 001 is that it is a cash item. It moves no EPS unless the accrual turns out too high, and then it comes back as the kind of prior-year release shown in 002. Either way it lands inside 2026-27.

Round-1 checks: baseline, the annual rollforward (+32.6bn in 2023, +16.7bn in 2024, -1.5bn in 2025) puts a +33.6bn half-year build on falling sales outside the range, and Q3 2025's +19bn quarter (old basis) is logged as a counterpoint. Window: H2 2026 and 2027. Not said: 0 hits for cash flow, list price, WAC or working capital on the Q2 2026 call. Priced in: the balance sheet is public, but the 6-K never sizes the timing and the guide's H2 arithmetic is never stated. Peer: Lilly +21.5% on revenue +51%. Units and scope: the 340B reversal is stripped out and FX is bounded at 2,686. Stacked assumptions: none in the base; the three-assumption 2027 figure is labelled illustrative and kept out of the base.
