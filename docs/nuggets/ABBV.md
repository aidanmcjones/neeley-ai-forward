## Sharpest Needle candidate

**ABBV-nugget-001: the Skyrizi royalty that adjusted EPS leaves out.** The story everyone tells is that Skyrizi refills the Humira gap. What goes unsaid is how much of each Skyrizi dollar AbbVie keeps. About 17 cents goes to a royalty that is booked as a contingent-consideration liability. AbbVie adds back every fair-value change, so the royalty never reaches cost of sales, adjusted gross margin (guided "above 84%"), adjusted operating margin (about 48.5%) or adjusted EPS. At the H1 2026 run-rate that is $3.6B a year, about $2.01 a share, or 14.5% of 2026 adjusted EPS guidance. Since the Q2 2026 10-Q, the royalty also reaches Skyrizi combination pipeline assets, the co-formulations AbbVie plans to launch in IBD, with payments now projected to 2044. So the next-generation franchise inherits the cost.

It passes every round-1 lesson:
- **Baseline:** the royalty rate is steady at 16% to 21% over 2023 to H1 2026, so it is structural, not an outlier year.
- **Window:** it costs $3.6B in 2026 and about $4.2B in 2027, and every combination readout (Phase 3 start targeted for H1 2028) can raise the GAAP charge.
- **Already said?** The CFO mentions "Skyrizi royalty payments" only as part of free cash flow. Across four calls, "contingent" gets 0 hits and "2044" gets 0 hits. No analyst has asked about it.
- **Priced in?** The cash figure is public. But the market multiple is applied to an adjusted EPS that excludes it, and the 2044 combination extension first appeared in the Q2 2026 10-Q; it is absent from the FY2025 10-K and the Q1 2026 10-Q.
- **Peer:** J&J's Tremfya royalty is about 5.0% and sits in cost of products sold. AbbVie's own Venclexta partner share also sits in cost of products sold.
- **Scope:** company-wide payments are divided by worldwide Skyrizi sales. The Nimble milestones (the only other addition) have not been paid.
- **Assumptions:** one (H2 2026 = H1 2026).

It beats the runner-up, the government-rebate shift (004), because 004 rests on a cause I infer (the Part D redesign) and treats all rebate pools as US. 001 is arithmetic on audited lines plus a new footnote.

## Part 2 hooks

- 001: Judge the Humira replacement on royalty-burdened economics. Prefer assets outside the Skyrizi royalty (wholly owned, such as the $10.9B Apogee IL-13 program) when sizing the next immunology bet. Do not count Skyrizi co-formulations as royalty-free growth.
- 002: Kill signal: the Q3 2026 10-Q (about Oct 31, 2026) fair-value charge and discount rate. A charge above $2B means the GAAP tax rate goes back above 30%.
- 004: Do not plan on Skyrizi and Rinvoq net-price erosion beyond the guided low single digits until the 2026 rollforward shows the government-rebate share stabilizing.
- 005: Kill signal: Vraylar US growth in Q1 2027. A decline steeper than about -15% (the Imbruvica path) argues for funding ABBV-932 over Vraylar promotion.
- 006: Kill signal: the CMS maximum fair price for Botox in the IPAY 2028 file. A cut above 50% puts more than $300M at risk.
- 007: Do not book the $100B pledge as new capex in a Part 2 budget. It largely restates existing spend.
- 008: Move Rinvoq earlier in the line of therapy through indications without a TNF-failure requirement (vitiligo, alopecia areata) rather than through more TNF-switch promotion.

## Rejected nuggets

- Humira US erosion (-34.7% in 2023, -41.3% in 2024, -57.1% in 2025, -49.4% in H1 2026, 10-K p.40, 10-Q p.28): this is the starter thread.
- Botox Cosmetic and aesthetics softness: the H1 2026 US +9.4% laps a 2025 loyalty-program price cut (10-Q p.29). Q2 US was -2.4% ($400M vs $410M) and US aesthetics in Q2 was -4.5% ($761M vs $797M). The gap is about $36M a quarter, below the 1% of operating income bar.
- Skyrizi vs Tremfya, Cosentyx and Entyvio gross-to-net: no company in the corpus discloses product-level net price. CMS gives only gross spend (logged in ai_caught).
- AbbVie prior-period rebate true-ups comparable to J&J's 3.0%: not disclosed (10-K p.47). Folded into 004 as a disclosure gap.
- Contingent consideration from Cerevel, ImmunoGen or Allergan: not supported. The 10-K ties every fair-value change to Skyrizi, and the Cerevel item is a $378M financing liability (logged).
- Imbruvica IRA price (US -37.6% in H1 2026): management names IRA pricing every quarter and J&J's file already covers it. Used only as the calibrator in 005.
- Rinvoq April 2037 settlement: in the 10-K, the September 2025 release and an analyst question. Used as the peer in 003.
- Apogee acquisition ($10.9B) and acquired IPR&D ($1,251M guided for 2026): one-off.
