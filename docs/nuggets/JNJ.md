## Sharpest Needle candidate

**Row 1, the second wave of US loss of exclusivity (JNJ-nugget-010).** Management's growth story is built on excluding STELARA ("96% of our business grew over 14%", Taubert, Q2 2026), and its only comment on the other products was that their generics are "not nearly as impactful as STELARA" (Wolk, Q4 2025). The sales schedules say otherwise. OPSUMIT, REMICADE, SIMPONI and IMBRUVICA were a stable US block for two years ($4,610M, $4,668M, $4,889M in 2023 to 2025, and -$1M year on year in Q3 2025), then lost $90M, $190M and $390M in the first three quarters of 2026, reaching 60% of STELARA's decline. Because STELARA has only $220M to $368M a quarter of US sales left, from Q1 2027 OPSUMIT, REMICADE and SIMPONI at their current rates ($321M) subtract more than STELARA could even if it went to zero. That is inside the 24-month window, every input is a verified schedule figure, and the one forward assumption (Q3 rates persist) is conservative because a US SIMPONI biosimilar is still pending. No call adds it up and no analyst has asked. Priced-in defense: the generic dates are public (Q4 2025 slides), but nobody sizes the four together or against STELARA, and BMY shows the alternative (a legacy subtotal). It beats the runner-up, the Genmab royalty (row 2), because row 2 was pitched by the CEO on the Q1 2026 call and pays off in 2029. It beats the rebate true-up (row 3) because row 3's sign is unknown.
