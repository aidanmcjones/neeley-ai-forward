## Sharpest Needle candidate

**LLY-nugget-002, the Jardiance milestones that stop after 2026.** The 10-K says Lilly may receive "up to $910 million ... in 2026" of sales-based milestones for Jardiance, and the 10-Q shows $500M booked in H1 2026 with up to $410M left. Jardiance revenue has carried a rising one-off every year: $300M in 2024 (9.0% of Jardiance revenue), $570M in 2025 (16.6%) and $500M in H1 2026 (21.3%). Because the right is limited to 2026, 2027 faces a step-down of up to $910M of near-100% margin revenue, or $0.81 of EPS (x 0.802 / 899.3M shares), 3.5% of pretax income, inside the 24-month window. The one assumption is that no new amendment refills 2027. Bull is $500M (only the H1 amount is earned). The FY2025 10-K also newly states that the main markets leave the collaboration after Dec 31, 2028.

Priced-in defense: the milestone is public, and the CFO named the $250M Q2 milestone in prepared remarks. The claim is narrower: the 2026-only limit and the absence of any 2027 successor are stated on no call, and the three-year pattern means 2026 growth in collaboration revenue is non-recurring. Limits: smaller than the other candidates, and Lilly could refill it, as it did with amendments in 2024 and 2025.

**LLY-nugget-001, the 2027 semaglutide set price on Mounjaro's Part D book, stays as the product tie row and is described as a sensitivity.** CMS shows Mounjaro had 5.1 million Part D claims and $6.3B of gross Part D spending in 2024, while semaglutide is priced by statute at $274 per 30 days from January 1, 2027, 78% below Ozempic's 2024 Part D gross cost per claim; Mounjaro's own per-claim gross ($1,241) equals Ozempic's ($1,245). The dollar figure is our choice, not a forecast: every $100 of extra concession per claim costs about $511M of revenue ($0.46 EPS). Do not show $511M as a forecast. It also leans on a third-party transcript quote ("small proportion") and on the semaglutide MFP, which is Novo's starter thread, so it fails as a needle.
