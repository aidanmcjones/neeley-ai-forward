## Sharpest Needle candidate

**GILD-nugget-001: Biktarvy's 2028 Medicare price and its spillover.**

What it says: management keeps telling investors there are "no major LOEs until 2036", and the JPM deck says the "LOE secured through April 2036". Yet the CMS file and Gilead's own 10-K show the largest product in the franchise gets a government price on January 1, 2028. Biktarvy is $11.5B of US sales, and its 2024 Part D gross spend was $3.46B, 6.4 times Dovato's. Gilead's own wording says the price will be "substantially lower". In May 2026 it widened the knock-on effects from Medicaid and 340B to "potentially other segments".

How it passes the round-1 checks:
- **Inside the window:** the price takes effect in 15 months. CMS should post the number around late November 2026, which is a kill signal six weeks out.
- **Not already said:** a grep of four transcripts and five decks finds no mention of the selection.
- **Priced-in defense:** the selection itself has been public since January 2026. What the market misses is that the cut is not confined to Medicare. Gilead says it can reach Medicaid, 340B and other segments, and Amgen's ENBREL shows that happening: its net price fell 22%, partly on 340B mix.
- **Peer column:** filled on both sides (ENBREL bear, Eliquis bull).
- **Assumptions:** two (Part D net base and discount depth), so confidence is medium.

Why it beats the runner-up, the ISL/LEN 50/50 split (002): 002 is contractual and an analyst asked about it, but its 24-month dollar effect depends on how fast a product that has not launched yet takes share. 001 has a fixed date, a fixed product and a government file behind it.

Caveat for the slide: Aidan's quant model already carries a Biktarvy IRA row. Present 001 as the verified upgrade to that row: the selection is confirmed, the LOE is April 2036 (not 2033), the spillover wording is new, and no price is asserted.

## Rejected nuggets

- CymaBay: the $3.8B acquired IPR&D charge (2024) and the $320M Janssen seladelpar royalty extinguishment (2025) are one-off charges.
- EVOKE-03 Trodelvy NSCLC impairment in Q2 2026 (code row GILD-forensics-385790) and the $590M bulevirtide impairments in 2025: one-off and already found by the code.
- Previously constrained IP-sale revenue: $400M (Q3 2025) and $156M (Q2 2026), booked almost entirely in Europe. It is noncash, management excludes it from illustrative EPS, and it is below the bar on its own. Watch for a third catch-up.
- Lakefront (formerly Galapagos): the $860M share of the Ouro upfront was booked as a reduction of acquired IPR&D, and Gilead pays 20% to 23% royalties on gamgertamig. The economics start after 2030, outside the window.
- Ouro contingent consideration ($277M liability): balance-sheet only; it is adjusted when milestones are met.
- Arcus: STAR-221 was discontinued, and the equity-investment risk is qualitative only. The 2025 continuation fee was $100M, below the bar.
- Section 232 tariffs (100% proclamation, April 2026): Gilead is exempt for three years if it keeps investing ($32B through 2030). There is no exposure figure in the corpus, so this is folded into 003.
- Part D wording in the 2026 10-Qs: start dates removed, substance unchanged (logged in ai_caught).
- HIV 340B and ADAP/Medicaid channel shares: not disclosed as numbers anywhere in the corpus (logged as an unsupported lead).
