## Sharpest Needle candidate

**BMY-nugget-001: the royalty floor drops out in 2027.** BMS's other-income line carried $2.7B of royalty income in 2025. The diabetes piece ($1.1B) ended Dec 31, 2025, and the CFO flagged it when he guided 2026 OI&E to about ($700M). The Keytruda and Tecentriq pieces end Dec 31, 2026 and are worth about $0.67B pretax, or $0.27 of EPS. They appear in no transcript: zero Keytruda, Tecentriq or Roche hits on four calls. It is the 2027 step that management has not quantified, yet in pretax terms it is 66% to 88% of the one it has quantified: the $1.5B to $2.0B Eliquis Europe step-down, of which BMS keeps only about half after Pfizer's share.

**How it passes the round-1 lessons.** Baseline: three years of Note 4 data show this is a scheduled end, not an outlier ($1,186M, $546M and $588M Keytruda royalties in 2023 to 2025 as the rate went from 6.5% to 2.5%). Inside the window: it hits 2027, starting in 10 weeks. Not already said: zero call mentions, while the diabetes expiry was stated, so the precedent shows management names a royalty cliff only when it guides the year. Priced-in defense: the schedule has been public since the 2017 settlement and is in the 10-K risk factor, but what goes unmentioned is its effect on 2027 OI&E (~($0.7B) to ~($1.37B)) and its size against the Eliquis step management does talk about. Peer column: reconciled to Merck's own 10-K within 1%, and Roche's Tecentriq sales are cited. Units and scope: the BMS 75% share is used, not Merck's 2.5% gross, and the royalty is global, so no geography mismatch. Stacked assumptions: one (H2 = H1).

**Why it beats the runner-up (002, Hengrui).** 002 is also verified, inside the window (Q3 results Oct 29, 2026) and unmentioned, but it is a one-time $0.24 charge of a kind BMS takes every year, and a skeptic will say every analyst already adjusts for BMS's IPRD exclusion. 001 is a permanent $0.27 a year step that no one on the calls has priced. Pair them on the slide: in BMS's 2026 to 2027 bridge, about $0.5 of EPS is either already contracted or already scheduled and sits outside both the headline guidance and the call narrative.

## Rejected nuggets

- Diabetes royalty end ($1,121M, Dec 31, 2025): real, but the CFO stated it in 2025Q4 prepared remarks and the 10-Q names it as an EPS driver (prepared_remarks rung). Used as context in 001.
- Eliquis Europe composition-of-matter and SPC expiry, Nov 2026, with a $1.5B to $2.0B 2027 step-down: management quantified it on two calls and analysts asked about it. Used only as the comparison in 001.
- Eliquis US loss of exclusivity Apr 2028 and Opdivo 2028: starter thread.
- Mirati CVR ($607M fair value of a ~$1.0B maximum; $336M charge in Q2 2025): fair-value changes are excluded from non-GAAP, so it is cash and GAAP only.
- Recurring IPRD impairments ($2.9B in 2024, $949M in 2025, $830M in H1 2026, including a radiopharmaceutical write-down): GAAP only and excluded from non-GAAP. Context, not a nugget.
- US Government Agreement (Eliquis free to Medicaid, tariff relief to Jan 2029, MFN-style launch pricing): terms are confidential ("The specific terms of the agreement remain confidential"), so there is no dollar path. Tariff relief ends after the 24-month window. Medicaid Eliquis net was already near zero from CPI penalties (Lenkowsky, 2025Q4).
- Reblozyl royalty to Merck (20% to 24% tiers, $505M in 2025): the effective rate moved only 21.7% to 22.4%, which is immaterial.
- Pomalyst IRA price (Jan 2027): generics arrive first, and management said it has no impact.
- Eliquis MFP per unit ($4.145 in 2026 vs 2024 Part D average spend of $9.637 per unit, -57%): Part D spend is pre-rebate, so the comparison overstates the cut. Folded into 003 as peer context only.
