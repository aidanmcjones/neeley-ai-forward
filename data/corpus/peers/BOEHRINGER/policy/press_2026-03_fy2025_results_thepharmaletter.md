Boehringer grows sales by 7.3% in a successful 2025

 Pharmaceutical 

25 March 2026

German family-owned pharma major Boehringer Ingelheim today reported financial results, showing that group sales rose by 7.3% to 27.8 billion euros ($31.4 billion) for the full year, supported by both the Human Pharma and Animal Health business units.

In 2025, Boehringer increased Research and Development (R&D) investments to 6.4 billion euros ($7.2 billion), representing 22.9% of group net sales.
