Key Takeaways

 Positive Sentiment: Takeda said fiscal 2025 was a strong year for its pipeline, with positive Phase III data for oveporexton, rusfertide and zasocitinib, plus positive pivotal results for TAK-881. Management framed these as setting up multiple near-term launches and a broader late-stage pipeline with significant long-term potential.

 Neutral Sentiment: FY2025 core revenue was about JPY 4.5 trillion and core operating profit was JPY 1.17 trillion , with results pressured by Vyvanse generic erosion but supported by disciplined cost control. Core EPS still grew 3.1% at constant currency, and free cash flow remained strong at JPY 684.5 billion.

 Positive Sentiment: The company highlighted three major launches expected within the next 12 months: oveporexton for narcolepsy type 1, rusfertide for polycythemia vera, and zasocitinib for psoriasis. Takeda said all three have priority review status or equivalent momentum and could become future blockbuster brands.

 Negative Sentiment: FY2026 guidance calls for low-single-digit revenue decline and core operating profit down 5% to 8% at constant exchange rates, as Takeda invests heavily in launches and R&D. The company also expects about JPY 170 billion of restructuring costs and roughly 4,500 roles impacted by its transformation program.

 Positive Sentiment: Management said its new transformation program should deliver more than JPY 200 billion of annualized gross savings by FY2028, supporting a return to growth and helping fund future launches. Takeda also plans to increase the annual dividend to JPY 204 per share and continue deleveraging toward a 2x net debt/EBITDA target.

About Takeda Pharmaceutical

 Takeda Pharmaceutical (NYSE:TAK) Company Limited is a global biopharmaceutical company headquartered in Tokyo, Japan. The company researches, develops, manufactures and commercializes prescription medicines and vaccines, with a focus on gastrointestinal and inflammatory diseases, rare diseases, plasma-derived therapies, oncology, neuroscience and vaccines.

Takeda’s portfolio includes treatments such as Entyvio for inflammatory bowel disease, Takhzyro for hereditary angioedema, Iclusig and Ninlaro for certain blood cancers, and Trintellix for major depressive disorder. The company also develops plasma-derived therapies and vaccines through its research and manufacturing operations.

Founded in Osaka in 1781 by Chobei Takeda I, Takeda has evolved from a traditional Japanese medicine business into a multinational biopharmaceutical company. Its medicines and services are available in Japan, the United States, Europe and other markets worldwide. Takeda’s global expansion was significantly strengthened by its acquisition of Shire plc, completed in 2019. Christophe Weber has served as the company’s president and chief executive officer since 2014.
