About BridgeBio Pharma

 BridgeBio Pharma (NASDAQ:BBIO) is a biotechnology company focused on discovering, developing and commercializing medicines for genetically driven diseases. The company uses its drug discovery and development platform to advance therapies across areas including rare diseases, genetic disorders and precision oncology, with programs designed to address the underlying biological causes of disease.

BridgeBio operates through a collection of subsidiary companies and development programs. Its portfolio has included therapies for cardiovascular, endocrine, neurological and oncological conditions. The company’s acoramidis, marketed in the United States as Attruby, was approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration in 2024 for the treatment of adults with cardiomyopathy caused by transthyretin-mediated amyloidosis.

Founded in 2015, BridgeBio is headquartered in Palo Alto, California, and conducts research, clinical development and commercial activities through its subsidiaries and collaborations in the United States and international markets. The company was co-founded and is led by Neil Kumar, who serves as chief executive officer. Its products and investigational therapies are developed for patients with serious conditions that often have limited or no approved treatment options.
