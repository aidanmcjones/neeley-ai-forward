therapy as part of their treatment journey. And we continue to strengthen the body of evidence supporting our recently launched multiple
myeloma therapies and combination regimens.

or death by 89% as early as the second line of treatment. In solid tumors, sales of RYBREVANT increased by 64%, reflecting its strength in
lung cancer. And by the end of the year, we expect to receive US FDA approval for RYBREVANT FASPRO in advanced head and neck
cancer, a disease with significant unmet needs.

eligible patients started on an INLEXZO regimen in the third quarter, demonstrating its growing role in transforming care in high-risk
non-muscle invasive disease.

degrader antibody conjugate platform that targets drivers of difficult to treat cancers, including KRAS. Our ambition is to become the number
one oncology company by 2030, with sales projected to exceed $50 billion.
