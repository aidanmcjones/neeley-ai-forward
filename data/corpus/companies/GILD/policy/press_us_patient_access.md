 Advancing Access® 

Gilead’s Advancing Access program helps patients understand their coverage and identify financial support options to access their Gilead HIV treatment and HIV prevention medication. The program offerings include:

 Access to program specialists who can help answer insurance-related questions and provide information regarding coverage options.

 Advancing Access Co-pay Savings Program, which provides co-pay support for eligible people on HIV treatment and HIV prevention medication with commercial insurance who need help paying for their out-of-pocket medicine costs. Eligible patients could pay as little as $0 per month.

 The Co-pay Savings Program is not available for people enrolled in government healthcare prescription drug programs, such as Medicare and Medicaid. Additional restrictions may apply.

The Advancing Access patient assistance program provides select Gilead medicines at no cost for qualified patients who meet the program's eligibility criteria.

 The program supports the following medicines: 

 Biktarvy® (bictegravir/emtricitabine/tenofovir alafenamide)*

Bixlenvo™ (bictegravir/lenacapavir)*

 Complera® (emtricitabine/rilpivirine/tenofovir disoproxil fumarate)

 Descovy® (emtricitabine/tenofovir alafenamide)*

 Emtriva® (emtricitabine)

 Genvoya® (elvitegravir/cobicistat/emtricitabine/tenofovir alafenamide)*

 Odefsey® (emtricitabine/rilpivirine/tenofovir alafenamide)*

 Stribild® (elvitegravir/cobicistat/emtricitabine/tenofovir disoproxil fumarate)*

 Sunlenca® (lenacapavir)*

 Truvada® (emtricitabine/tenofovir disoproxil fumarate)

 Tybost® (cobicistat)

Yeztugo® (lenacapavir)* 

*Medication offered through the Patient Assistance Program/Medication Assistance Program (PAP/MAP) 

To learn more about the Advancing Access program, visit
 www.GileadAdvancingAccess.com or call 1-800-226-2056, Monday through Friday between 9 a.m. and 8 p.m. EST. 

 Cayston Access Program® 

The Cayston Access Program® helps patients understand their coverage and identify financial support options to access Cayston® (aztreonam for inhalation solution). The program offerings include:

Access to case managers who can help answer insurance-related questions and provide information regarding coverage options.

Information about authorized distributors (i.e. participating specialty pharmacies).

Cayston Co-pay Savings Program, which provides co-pay support for eligible patients with commercial insurance who need help paying for their out-of-pocket medicine costs. Eligible patients could pay as little as $10 per month. The Co-pay Savings Program is not available for people enrolled in government healthcare prescription drug programs, such as Medicare Part D and Medicaid. 

Cayston patient assistance program, which provides Cayston at no cost for qualified patients who meet the program’s eligibility criteria.

To learn more about the Cayston Access Program, visit https://www.cayston.com/ or call 1-877-7CAYSTON (877-722-9786), Monday through Friday between 8 a.m. and 5 p.m. EST.

 Letairis® Co-pay Savings Program 

Letairis Co-pay Savings Program, provides co-pay support for eligible patients with commercial insurance who need help paying for their out-of-pocket medicine costs. Eligible patients could pay as little as $5 per month. The Co-pay Savings Program is not available for people enrolled in government healthcare prescription drug programs, such as Medicare, Medicaid, TriCare, or DoD. Additional restrictions may apply. 

To enroll in the Letairis Co-pay Savings Program, call 1-866-664-5327, Monday through Friday between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m. EST. 

 Support Path® 

The Support Path® program helps patients understand their coverage and identify financial support options to access their Gilead Hepatitis C (HCV), Hepatitis B (HBV), Hepatitis D (HDV), and Primary Biliary Cholangitis (PBC) medicine. The program offerings include: 

Access to program specialists who can help answer insurance-related information and provide questions regarding coverage options.

Access to the Hepatitis D Nurse Educator Program.

Gilead HCV, HBV, HDV and PBC Co-pay Savings Programs, which provides co-pay support for eligible patients with commercial insurance who need help paying for their out-of-pocket medicine costs. Eligible HCV patients could pay as little as $5 per month. Eligible HBV, HDV and PBC patients could pay as little as $0 per month. The Co-pay Savings Program is not available for patients enrolled in government healthcare prescription drug programs, such as Medicare Part D and Medicaid. Additional restrictions may apply. 

Support Path patient assistance program, which provides Gilead HCV, HBV, HDV and PBC medicines at no cost for qualified patients who meet the program’s eligibility criteria.

The Support Path program supports the following medicines:

Epclusa® (sofosbuvir/velpatasvir)*

Hepcludex®(bulevirtide-gmod)* 

Harvoni® (ledipasvir/sofosbuvir)*

Livdelzi® (seladelpar)*

Sovaldi® (sofosbuvir)

Vosevi® (sofosbuvir/velpatasvir/voxilaprevir)*

Vemlidy® (tenofovir alafenamide)*

*Medication offered through the Support Path Patient Assistance Program

To learn more about Support Path, visit https://www.mysupportpath.com or call 1-855-769-7284, Monday through Friday between 9 a.m. and 8 p.m. EST.

Gilead Oncology Co-pay Savings Program, which provides co-pay support for eligible patients with commercial insurance who need help paying for their out-of-pocket medicine costs.
