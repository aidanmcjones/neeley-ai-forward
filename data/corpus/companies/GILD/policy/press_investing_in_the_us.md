Shaping Tomorrow’s Biopharma Innovation , Right from the Bay Area 

We are expanding our Foster City campus with a state of the art, New Technical Development Center (NTDC). Designed to serve as a pivotal hub for biologics research and development, the NTDC accelerates our progress on transformative therapies and fuels the next wave of American-made science. This investment underscores our mission: to power discovery and create opportunity, right here at home, for patients everywhere.  Learn more .

U.S. Breakthroughs Worldwide Impact 

From our headquarters in Foster City, California, we pursue world-class science to tackle some of the greatest public health challenges. With deep roots in American innovation, we are building more than medicine — we’re building opportunity, health and hope.

Through $32 billion in planned U.S. investments, we’re strengthening our domestic footprint to power the next era of discovery, job creation and public health preparedness — while continuing to invest globally to ensure patients everywhere benefit from our science.

 Engineering a New Era of Innovation

 Science Powered, AI Fueled-Future Ready. 

Innovation is at the heart of all we do. That’s why we’re continually advancing how we discover, develop and deliver the next generation of medicines across our therapeutic areas of virology, oncology and inflammation. 

From collaborating with biotech startups across the U.S. to integrating AI into our biopharma campuses, our investments are shaping a new standard in American 
science and infrastructure.

 Our Model for the Future

 Investing at home. Uplifting U.S. Communities. 

More than 80% of Gilead’s global profits are reinvested in the U.S. — supporting scientific advances, workforce development and healthier communities. With most of our intellectual property based in America and operations anchored in U.S. communities, we are ensuring that tomorrow’s life-changing therapies are discovered, 
developed and delivered close to home.
