TAKEAWAYS

 Total Revenue -- nearly $17 billion, reflecting 10.2% growth driven by the performance of the immunology and neuroscience portfolios.

 Adjusted EPS -- $3.65, exceeding the guidance midpoint by $0.06 despite a $0.17 unfavorable impact from acquired IPR&D expense.

 Full-Year Revenue Guidance -- approximately $67.6 billion, an increase of 300 million from the prior forecast.

 Full-Year Adjusted EPS Guidance -- $13.87 to $14.07, incorporating $0.14 of anticipated dilution related to the Apogee Therapeutics acquisition.

 SKYRIZI Sales -- $5.5 billion, representing 24% operational growth led by psoriasis market share gains and inflammatory bowel disease demand.

 RINVOQ Sales -- more than $2.5 billion, a 23.7% operational increase supported by 30% growth in the gastroenterology segment.

 Neuroscience Revenue -- more than $3.2 billion, up 20% on an operational basis with double-digit growth across its three primary pillars.

 Immunology Revenue -- nearly $8.8 billion, an operational increase of 14.6% as growth in newer therapies offset biosimilar competition.

 HUMIRA Sales -- $706 million, declining 36.1% operationally due to biosimilar competition in line with management expectations.

 Vraylar Sales -- nearly $1.1 billion, increasing 19% based on share gains in bipolar disorder and major depressive disorder.

 Oncology Revenue -- more than $1.6 billion, down 2.4% operationally as growth in newer assets was offset by IMBRUVICA declines.

 VENCLEXTA Sales -- $771 million, up 9.6% operationally due to its use as a preferred fixed-duration treatment for chronic lymphocytic leukemia.

 IMBRUVICA Sales -- down 29.4%, reflecting pricing impacts from the Inflation Reduction Act and competitive share pressure.

 Aesthetics Revenue -- nearly $1.3 billion, declining 0.9% operationally due to continued dermal filler market headwinds.

 Botox Cosmetic Sales -- $728 million, up 3.4% operationally driven by modest global market growth.

 Juvederm Sales -- $245 million, down 6.6% operationally as specific dermal filler markets faced continued challenges.

 Adjusted Gross Margin -- 84.7% of sales, with a full-year forecast of above 84%.

 Adjusted Operating Margin -- 48.3% of sales, which includes a 1.7 percentage point unfavorable impact from acquired IPR&D.

 R&D Expense -- $9.8 billion forecast for the full year, an increase of 100 million to support Apogee-related investments.

 Net Interest Expense -- $2.9 billion forecast for the full year, reflecting a 200 million increase for partial-year financing of the Apogee transaction.

 SKYRIZI Revenue Guidance -- $21.7 billion for the full year, raised by 100 million due to momentum in psoriatic and IBD indications.

 Vialev Sales -- $256 million, representing a 27% increase on a sequential basis as the product tracks toward blockbuster status.

RISKS

Jeffrey Ryan Stewart noted that IMBRUVICA sales declined 29.4% due to "IRA pricing and competitive share pressure," identifying legislative pricing adjustments as a specific drag on oncology revenue.

Jeffrey Ryan Stewart stated that Juvederm sales fell 6.6% operationally, "reflecting continued headwinds in key dermal filler markets," which represents a sustained challenge for the aesthetics business.

SUMMARY

Management reported that second quarter performance exceeded internal expectations, which led to a 300 million increase in full-year revenue guidance for AbbVie Inc. ( ABBV +0.71% ) . The company announced the acquisition of Apogee Therapeutics, which the CFO stated will contribute approximately $0.14 of dilution to the 2026 adjusted EPS outlook. Growth was concentrated in the immunology and neuroscience segments, which helped mitigate the impact of biosimilar competition for HUMIRA and the pricing pressures affecting IMBRUVICA. The company also secured several regulatory milestones, including European approvals for the oral migraine treatment QLIPTA and the fast-acting aesthetics toxin Bowie.

CEO Michael indicated the Apogee acquisition represents an opportunity to "bolster AbbVie's leading immunology portfolio" and support performance goals through the 2030s.

Management projected collective peak sales for oral CGRPs to exceed $5 billion, following the expanded European approval of QLIPTA for acute migraine treatment.

CSO Thakkar noted that SKYRIZI subcutaneous induction for Crohn’s disease demonstrated endoscopic response rates "45 points higher in patients who had not previously experienced advanced therapy."

The company expects its Parkinson’s disease portfolio, including the anticipated launch of tevapadon, to reach collective peak sales of more than $5 billion.

CFO Reents stated the company remains committed to achieving a net leverage ratio of 2 times within two to three years following the close of the Apogee transaction.

Management reported that SKYRIZI has achieved market share leadership in more than 30 countries, capturing new patient starts at a rate fourfold higher than competitors in the U.S. psoriasis market.

CSO Thakkar highlighted that the blood-brain barrier crossing antibody ABBV-1.76 thousand demonstrated cerebrospinal fluid penetration over 1%, which is severalfold higher than standard antibodies.

INDUSTRY GLOSSARY

 ADC : Antibody-drug conjugate; a class of biopharmaceutical drugs designed as a targeted therapy for treating cancer.

 IBD : Inflammatory bowel disease; a group of inflammatory conditions of the colon and small intestine.

 CGRP : Calcitonin gene-related peptide; a protein involved in migraine pathophysiology that is targeted by certain migraine medications.

 IRA : Inflation Reduction Act; U.S. legislation that includes provisions for government negotiation of drug prices.

 CLL : Chronic lymphocytic leukemia; a type of cancer of the blood and bone marrow.

 BPDCN : Blastic plasmacytoid dendritic cell neoplasm; an ultra-rare and aggressive form of blood cancer.

 HS : Hidradenitis suppurativa; a chronic inflammatory skin condition.

 NBRx : New-to-brand prescriptions; a metric tracking patients starting a specific medication for the first time or switching to it.
