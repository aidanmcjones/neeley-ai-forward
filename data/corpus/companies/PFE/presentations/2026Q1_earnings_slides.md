Deliver on Critical R&D Milestones - Recent Successes
• Tilrekimig (IL-4/IL-13/TSLP trispecific): Demonstrated significant increase vs. placebo in 
percentage of participants achieving EASI-75, in Phase 2 atopic dermatitis study
• Next-gen PCV: Dosed first patient in Phase 3 pediatric study of PCV25; adult program 
advancing directly to 5th-generation vaccine candidate covering 35 serotypes
• PADCEV*: Reduced the risk of recurrence or death by nearly 50% in patients with 
cisplatin-eligible MIBC in Phase 3 EV-304 study; sBLA accepted for FDA priority review
• ELREXFIO: Significantly improved PFS for DCE patients with relapsed or refractory 
multiple myeloma who received ≥1 prior line of treatment, in Phase 3 study**
• Atirmociclib (CDK4i): Showed 40% reduction in the risk of disease progression or death in 
patients with HR+, HER2- mBC and prior CDK4/6 inhibitor therapy (Phase 2)
